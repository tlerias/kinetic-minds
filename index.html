Kinetic Minds

Look what we built.

Kinetic Minds — a creative tech studio for kids in Montclair, NJ — closed its doors after our final classes on Saturday, May 16, 2026. Before we go, here’s what this community made together.

Six months ago, we asked Montclair for a community fundraiser to pilot one coding cohort. The plan was small, specific, and a little nervous.

The original ask

  • $3,750 community fundraiser
  • One six-week pilot cohort
  • Eight kids in Montclair
  • Up to two need-based scholarship seats, if the math worked

One pilot. Eight kids. Two sponsored seats. That was the plan.

In six months, together, we built something so much bigger.

What we built together

  • Recurring weekend classes families came back to
  • LEGO robotics, Sphero, coding, and creative builds
  • Birthday parties parents requested by name
  • A real team, paid through real payroll
  • Curriculum, observational assessments, and kit systems
  • Real conversations with schools and Girl Scout troops about what came next

And the most important part

30% of seats were sponsored.

We didn’t just remove barriers to access. We exceeded our own goal for it.

That doesn’t happen without you.

Thank you for trusting us with your kids. They are the reason any of this mattered.
